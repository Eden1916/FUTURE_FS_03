Our Story

From Addis Ababa
to Your Neighbourhood

Dembel was born in 2015, when founder Selam Tadesse brought her grandmother's coffee tradition across the world. In Ethiopia, coffee isn't a drink — it's a ceremony, a gathering, a way of welcoming guests.

We import single-origin beans directly from Yirgacheffe and Sidamo farms, roast them in-house every morning, and brew them the traditional way in a clay jebena pot. Our kitchen serves injera, doro wat, tibs, and the flavours of home.

  • ✅ Single-origin Ethiopian beans, direct from farmers
  • ✅ Traditional jebena brewing & coffee ceremony
  • ✅ Injera made fresh daily from teff flour
  • ✅ Vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free options available

The Buna Ceremony

Coffee is Not
a Drink. It's a Welcome.

In Ethiopia, the coffee ceremony — buna — is sacred. Green beans are washed, roasted over coals, ground by hand, and brewed in a clay jebena. The aroma of roasting beans and burning frankincense fills the room.

The ceremony has three rounds, each with its own name:

Abol The first pour — strongest and most flavourful
Huletegna The second pour — smoother and mellow
Bereka The third pour — a blessing to close

We hold live ceremonies every weekend. Book ahead — space is limited and the experience takes about 45 minutes.
