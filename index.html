Our Story

Born from a Love
of Good Coffee

Brew & Bloom started as a weekend pop-up in 2017. Owner and head barista Priya Nair had one goal: bring the kind of thoughtful, slow coffee she'd discovered travelling through Melbourne and Copenhagen to her own neighbourhood.

Seven years later, we still roast our beans in small batches, bake everything before 6 AM, and greet most of our regulars by name.

  • ✅ Single-origin beans, sourced directly from farmers
  • ✅ All pastries baked fresh on-site every morning
  • ✅ Oat, almond, soy & coconut milk available
  • ✅ Dog-friendly outdoor seating
